Leadership

How to Win Friends and Influence People

Dale Carnegie

Timeless guidance on listening, empathy, and building genuine relationships.

Start with Why

Simon Sinek

A reminder to lead with purpose first, so teams and customers can follow.

Fierce Conversations

Susan Scott

Straightforward guidance on honest conversations that move relationships and work forward.

Atomic Habits

Small, steady habits that compound into lasting change.

Instant Influence

A classic case for design that matches how people actually think and act.

A clear framework for messaging that puts the customer at the center of the story.
